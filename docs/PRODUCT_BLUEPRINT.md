# 999XXX Product Blueprint

## Consumer
Home/discovery, search, categories, creator profiles, video detail/player shell, favorites, history,
account, subscriptions/purchases, reporting and takedown request entry.

## Creator
Creator application, identity/age verification status, performer roster, rights documents,
content submission, licensing metadata, earnings/payout ledger and takedown requests.

## Admin
Creator verification queue, content rights gate, moderation queue, reports/takedowns,
audit trail, account controls and publishing approval.

## Publishing rule
A submission must never become publishable until required creator/performer verification,
consent and commercial distribution rights records are approved.

## Production integrations still required
Secure object/video storage + transcoding/CDN, identity/age-verification provider,
adult-compatible payment processor, email/SMS provider, moderation systems and legal/compliance review.
